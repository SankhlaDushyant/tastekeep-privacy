TasteKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03

Who runs TasteKeep

TasteKeep is a private recipe app for one family and invited friends. It is run by an individual, not a company, and is shared through Google Play internal testing. It is not sold, and it has no ads.

What the app collects

  • Your email address. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. There is no password.
  • Your recipes. Titles, ingredients, steps, notes, collections, grocery and pantry lists, and the web address a recipe came from.
  • Photos. Photos you take or choose of recipes, and the photos you take while cooking.
  • Household membership. The name you choose to show in your household and which household you belong to.

The app asks for camera access so you can photograph recipes and cooking steps. It does not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers for advertising.

Where your data goes

  • Supabase. Your account, recipes, photos and household details are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, so your household can share them. Only members of your household can read them.
  • Claude (Anthropic). When you ask TasteKeep to read a recipe from photos, a PDF, a document, a web link or pasted text, that content is sent through a TasteKeep server function to Anthropic's Claude to turn it into a recipe. We do not keep the content on that function; we record who asked, when, the type of content, and its size and usage counts, never the content itself. Anthropic handles what it receives under its own policy.
  • Recipe websites. When you import from a link, your phone fetches that public web page directly, like a browser would.

What stays on your phone

TasteKeep keeps an offline copy of your recipes on your phone. Translating a recipe is done on-device with Google ML Kit: once its language files are downloaded, the text being translated is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.

How long we keep things

Recipes you delete in the app are marked deleted and no longer shown, but may remain in the database until the owner removes them. Photos you take while cooking are removed from storage when that cooking session ends. Recipe photos (scans and photos added to recipes) stay in storage until deletion is requested, even if you remove them in the app. Supabase also keeps its own sign-in and server logs, which can include IP addresses.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data deleted, email sankhla.dushyant@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. Tell me what you want removed and I will do it by hand.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change too.
